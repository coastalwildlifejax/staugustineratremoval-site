Signs You Have Rats

Roof rats are common across St. Augustine. If you're seeing any of these, they're already inside.

🔊

Scratching at night

Running or scratching in the attic, ceiling or walls — usually after dark.

💩

Droppings

Dark, pellet-shaped droppings in the attic, garage, pantry or under sinks.

⚡

Chewed wires

Gnawed wiring, AC lines and insulation — a real fire and repair risk.

🐾

Rub marks & trails

Greasy smudges along beams, pipes and entry points where they travel.

👃

Odor

A musky ammonia smell — or worse, a dead rat in a wall or attic.

🌴

Activity in palms & roofline

Rats running along fences, palm trees, power lines or the roof edge.

Common Questions

How much does rat removal cost?

It depends on the size of the home and how many entry points need sealing. The inspection is free, and you get a clear, written price before we start.

Do you use poison?

Not inside your home. Poisoned rats often die in walls and attics, causing odor problems. We remove them with trapping and stop new ones by sealing entry points.

How long does it take?

Exclusion work is usually completed in a day or two, with trap checks over the following visits until activity stops.

Will they come back?

Not through the areas we seal — that's what the warranty covers. Sealing entry points is the only permanent fix.

What areas do you serve?

All of St. Johns County, including Downtown St. Augustine, St. Augustine Beach, Ponte Vedra, Nocatee, Vilano Beach, World Golf Village, St. Johns and Palm Coast.
